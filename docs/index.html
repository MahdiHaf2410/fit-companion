Fit Companion is a training log, a food diary and a coach in one Android app. It works offline, speaks Arabic and English, and knows what kesra and chorba are.

What's inside

The app has four tabs. Here is what each one does for you.

Workouts

Build your own programs or start from a template, then log every set as you go. The rest timer runs in your notifications, and the app spots new records and tells you when to add weight.

  • 420 exercises with Arabic names and a demo video for each
  • Supersets, drop sets, warm-ups and effort (RPE)
  • Warns you when it is time for a lighter week

Nutrition

Log meals from a food list that includes Algerian dishes, scan a barcode, or take a photo of your plate and let the AI count it. You can also just say what you ate.

  • Calories and protein, carbs and fat against your goal
  • Your real maintenance calories, worked out from your weight trend
  • Water tracking and meal reminders

Progress

Weight, body fat and measurements on clear charts, and before and after photos you can compare side by side. Connect Health Connect to see how your sleep changes your training.

  • Weekly and monthly challenges with levels to reach
  • A leaderboard with friends you add by code
  • Share a workout or a level as an image

Coach

Ask the coach anything about your training or food, with a photo or a video if it helps. It reads a short summary of your numbers, never your name or email.

  • A weekly report with a score and three goals
  • A program built from the exercises in the app
  • A full day of meals that fits your targets, with a shopping list

A coach that has read your log

Most apps give you the same tips as everyone else. This one answers from your own week: what you lifted, what you ate and how you slept.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid plan. The AI features have a daily limit so they stay free for everyone.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. If you create an account, a backup is kept online that only you can read. Friends see your level and weekly totals, nothing else.

Does it need the internet?

Not for logging workouts, food or weight. The coach, the demo videos, barcode lookup and backups need a connection.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Android warns me about an unknown app. Why?

The app is installed from this site instead of the Play Store, so Android shows a warning. The full source code is public on GitHub if you want to check it.
