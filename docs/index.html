Workouts

Every set, logged in one tap.

Tick a set and the rest timer starts in your notifications. Lift more than ever before and the app tells you straight away.

  • Your own programs, or start from a template
  • Supersets, drop sets, warm-ups and effort (RPE)
  • Tells you when to add weight, and when to rest
Exercises

420 exercises, each with a video.

Search in Arabic or English, filter by muscle or by what your gym has, and watch how it is done before your first rep.

  • Chest to neck, plus cardio
  • Barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable, kettlebell, band
  • Add your own exercises too
Nutrition

Take a photo. It counts the calories.

Photograph your plate, scan a barcode, or just say what you ate in Darija. Algerian dishes are already in the list.

  • Protein, carbs and fat against your goal
  • Your real maintenance calories, from your weight trend
  • Water tracking and meal reminders
Progress

See the change, week by week.

Weight, body fat and measurements on clear charts, and before and after photos side by side. Connect Health Connect to see how sleep moves your numbers.

  • Progress photos with dates
  • Body measurements and body fat
  • Sleep compared with your training
AI coach

A coach that has read your log.

It answers from your own week: what you lifted, what you ate and how you slept. It never sees your name or email.

  • Weekly report with a score and three goals
  • A program built from the app's exercises
  • A full day of meals with a shopping list
Challenges

Level up with your friends.

Weekly and monthly challenges give you XP. Add friends with a code and see who lifted the most this week.

  • Levels from Beginner to Legend
  • A leaderboard only your friends can see
  • Share a workout or a level as an image

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid plan. The AI features have a daily limit so they stay free for everyone.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. If you create an account, a backup is kept online that only you can read. Friends see your level and weekly totals, nothing else.

Does it need the internet?

Not for logging workouts, food or weight. The coach, the demo videos, barcode lookup and backups need a connection.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Android warns me about an unknown app. Why?

The app is installed from this site instead of the Play Store, so Android shows a warning. The full source code is public on GitHub if you want to check it.
